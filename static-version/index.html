Questions

Frequently asked

Can I still sell on other platforms?

Absolutely. BuyBooks doesn't require exclusivity. Many authors keep their marketplace listings for discovery and send their fans to their BuyBooks store to earn more per copy.

How are my earnings calculated?

You set the retail price. We subtract the printing cost (for physical books) and payment processing — everything left over is yours. We never take a percentage of your sale.

What book sizes and bindings do you support?

All popular trim sizes from pocket paperbacks to large-format coffee-table books, in paperback or hardcover, with matte or gloss covers and cream or white paper.

Do I need to handle printing or shipping?

No. Each order is printed on demand and shipped straight to your reader. You never touch inventory unless you want to order author copies.

Which countries can I sell to?

Readers in over 150 countries can buy from your store, and our print partners across North America, Europe, Africa and Asia keep shipping times short.

What if BuyBooks isn't right for me?

Let us know within 30 days of signing up and we'll refund your subscription in full.
